Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how this Discord bot ("Bot") handles information when used in our private Discord server.

1. Information We Collect

The Bot may access limited Discord information necessary to provide its features, including:

  • Discord user IDs
  • Discord server IDs
  • Usernames or display names when required by a Bot feature
  • Server configuration and Bot settings

The Bot does not intentionally collect passwords, payment information, or other sensitive personal information.

2. How Information Is Used

Information accessed by the Bot is used only to:

  • Provide and operate the Bot's features
  • Perform commands requested by server members
  • Maintain server-specific settings
  • Help prevent misuse or unauthorized use of the Bot

3. Data Sharing

Because the Bot is intended for a private server, information accessed by the Bot is not sold or shared for advertising purposes.

Information may only be sent to third-party services when necessary for a specific Bot feature.

4. Data Storage and Retention

The Bot only stores information that is necessary for its operation. Information may be retained for as long as it is needed to provide the Bot's features or maintain the private server. Information that is no longer needed may be deleted.

5. Data Deletion

If you believe the Bot has stored information associated with you that should be deleted, contact the server owner or Bot developer through the private Discord server.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information handled by the Bot. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

7. Third-Party Services

The Bot operates through Discord and may use other services required for specific features. Those services may have their own privacy policies and terms. The Bot is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services.

8. Children's Privacy

The Bot does not intentionally collect personal information from children. Users should use Discord in accordance with Discord's own age requirements and policies.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Bot's features or data practices change. Any updated version will be made available through the Bot's designated Privacy Policy page.

10. Contact

For questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, contact the server owner or Bot developer through the private Discord server.

11. Acceptance

By using the Bot in the private Discord server, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
